# System Requirements Specification: Lean 4 Formal Verification of Prime Definitions, Composite Emergence Boundaries, 0.5-Pitch Structure, and Interval Propositions

## 1. Overview and Background

This requirements specification defines the formal proof structure and verification criteria for the Lean 4 codebase under the `NaturalMathematics` namespace.

Without introducing any external unproved axioms or `sorry`, this formalization relies exclusively on the standard axioms of Lean 4 (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`). It establishes the logical chain below and formally demonstrates the fact that Lean 4 passes the propositional form directly from the preceding proved theorems:

1. **Prime Definition and Emergence Boundary (The `1-2` Boundary)**: Within the natural number sequence starting from the Peano origin ($1$), the smallest prime $2$ ($2^1$) emerges.
2. **Composite Definition and Emergence Boundary (The `1-2-3-4` Boundary)**: Composite numbers (integers $\ge 2$ that are not prime) never emerge in the range $m \le 3$ (`1-2-3`), and first appear only upon reaching $4 = 2^2$ (`1-2-3-4`).
3. **Necessity of Pitch and Connection to the 0.5-Pitch**: From the boundary between $2^1 = 2$ (prime) and $2^2 = 4$ (composite) together with the preceding lemmas, the framework of the $0.5$-pitch exponent progression ($1 \to 1.5 \to 2$) and the $2n - 2$ baseline is established.
4. **Propositional Verification and Remaining Scope**: Building upon quadrilateral and square interval partitioning/nesting inequalities, the partition identity and equivalence between cumulative primes $\pi(n^2)$ and cumulative composites $C(n^2)$, and logarithmic scaling equilibrium, it is established as a verified fact that Lean 4 passes the propositional form solely from these proved theorems. Structurally, the only remaining element is the acceleration of prime counts via the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion.

## 2. Functional and Proof Requirements (Lean 4 Specification)

### 2.1 Step 1: Peano Origin and 1D Synchronized Infinite Tree

* **`synchronized_infinity_principle`**: Proves the injectivity of $f(x) = x + 1$ and the non-reachability of $0$ in its image, establishing the foundation for synchronized infinite progression from origin $1$.
* **`SynchronizedInfiniteTree1D` / `generation1D` / `generation_sync_1d_lt_value`**: Inductively defines the 1D synchronized tree advancing from `root` ($1$) via `step`, and proves by induction that the generation depth `generation1D t < n` holds for every node $t$.
* **`ramanujacharyulu_synchronized_1d_tree`**: Proves that for any $n \ge 1$, there exists a tree node $t$ satisfying `generation1D t = n - 1 ∧ generation1D t < n`.

### 2.2 Step 2: Prime/Composite Emergence Boundaries, Preliminary 0.5-Pitch Lemmas, Interval Structure, and Baseline Equivalence

* **`IsComposite` / `primeCount` / `compositeCount` / `prime_plus_composite_eq`**:
  * Defines `IsComposite m` as `2 ≤ m ∧ ¬ Nat.Prime m`.
  * Proves the partition identity $\pi(m) + C(m) = m - 1$ for cumulative prime count $\pi(m)$ and cumulative composite count $C(m)$ for all $m \ge 2$.
* **`no_composite_in_peano_three`**: Proves $\forall m \le 3,\ \neg \text{IsComposite } m$, establishing that no composite numbers arise within the `1-2-3` boundary.
* **`composite_emergence_requires_four`**: Proves $(\exists m \le N,\ \text{IsComposite } m) \leftrightarrow 4 \le N$, establishing the equivalence between composite emergence and reaching `1-2-3-4` ($4 = 2^2$).
* **`composite_generation_pitch`**: Jointly proves non-emergence for $m \le 3$, `IsComposite 4`, the lower bound $4 \le c$ for any composite $c$, and the boundary spans $4 - 3 = 1$ and $4 - 2 = 2$.
* **`prime_definition_forces_half_pitch_progression`**: For any $2 \le n$, proves `1-2-3` non-compositeness, compositeness and minimality of $2^2 = 4$, the existence of prime $3$ strictly between $2^1$ and $2^2$ ($2^1 < 3 < 2^2$), $n^1 < n^2$ with $2 \le n^2 - n^1$, boundary spans ($4 - 3 = 1$, $4 - 2 = 2$), the $0.5$-pitch progression ($1 + 0.5 = 1.5$, $1.5 + 0.5 = 2$, $1 < 1.5$, $1.5 < 2$), and `IsComposite (n ^ 2)`.
* **`quadrilateral_theorem`**: For $2 \le n$, proves the partitions $n^2 = n(n - 1) + n$ and $n(n + 1) = n^2 + n$, along with the strict nesting inequalities:
  $$
  (n - 1)^2 < n(n - 1) < n^2 < n(n + 1) < (n + 1)^2
  $$
* **`baseline_2n_sub_2_equivalence`**: For $2 \le n$, proves the equivalence:
  $$
  2n - 2 \le \pi(n^2) \iff C(n^2) \le (n - 1)^2
  $$
* **`structural_capacity_equilibrium` / `capacity_equilibrium_holds`**: For $2 \le n$, proves $0 < \ln n$ and $\ln(n^2) / \ln n = 2$.

### 2.3 Step 3: Propositional Dependency Structure and Instantiation

* **`OppermannQuadraticIntervalProp n` / `LegendreSquareIntervalProp n`**:
  * `OppermannQuadraticIntervalProp n`: The proposition asserting the existence of primes in both intervals $(n(n-1), n^2)$ and $(n^2, n(n+1))$.
  * `LegendreSquareIntervalProp n`: The proposition asserting the existence of a prime in the square interval $(n^2, (n+1)^2)$.
* **`step3_dependency_structure`**: A propositional-form theorem that takes the proved theorems of Step 1 and Step 2 as explicit dependencies and derives `OppermannQuadraticIntervalProp n → LegendreSquareIntervalProp (n - 1) ∧ LegendreSquareIntervalProp n` via the quadrilateral and square nesting inequalities.
* **`step3_with_oppermann_and_legendre`**: Instantiates `step3_dependency_structure` directly with the concrete theorems proved in Step 1 and Step 2, jointly establishing the 1D synchronized tree, compositeness of $n^2$, the $2n - 2$ baseline equivalence, the interval inequalities, logarithmic equilibrium, and the interval proposition bridge.

## 3. Non-Functional and Verification Requirements

1. **Zero Custom Axioms and Zero `sorry`**: The codebase must contain no custom `axiom` declarations or `sorry` placeholders, and `#print axioms` output must depend strictly on the standard Lean 4 axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`).
2. **Clarity of Propositional Verification and Remaining Scope**: The specification must explicitly record both the fact that Lean 4 passes the propositional form from the preceding proved theorems and that the sole remaining component is prime count acceleration via the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion.
3. **Compilation Integrity**: All definitions and theorems must compile cleanly without type errors under `set_option autoImplicit false`.
